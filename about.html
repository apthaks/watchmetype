watchmetype

watchmetype replays your post keystroke by keystroke, so anyone can see it was typed, not pasted.

how it works

Write a post of up to 500 characters. Pasting is switched off, so every word has to be typed. Tap share and you get your text plus a link.

Anyone who opens the link watches the post being typed at your pace: every pause, typo, deletion and rewrite. Long pauses show as “paused 3 min” instead of making the reader wait.

typed, not pasted

Copy-pasted and AI-generated text arrives all at once. Real typing hesitates, backtracks and fixes itself. A replay shows the words arriving one keystroke at a time, so a reader can judge for themselves how the post was written.

It’s a witness, not a proof. A replay shows the text was typed. It can’t show who typed it, or rule out someone retyping words from somewhere else.

private by design

Your post lives in the link and nowhere else. The whole recording is packed into the part of the address after the #, which browsers never send to a server. There are no accounts, no database, no tracking and no analytics.

That also means a shared link can’t be edited or deleted. In the same browser, opening your own link shows “continue editing”: new edits are added to the recording and you get a new link. The old link keeps showing the old version.

questions

Does it prove I didn’t use AI?

Not on its own. It shows a human-paced typing session with real pauses and corrections, which is hard to fake but not impossible.

Does it work with swipe typing and other languages?

Yes. It records what changes in the text, not which keys were pressed, so swipe, autocomplete, emoji and input methods for languages like Japanese or Hindi all replay as they happened. A word inserted at once by swipe appears at once.

Why is the link so long?

Because the whole recording is inside it. A 500-character post makes a link of about 1–2 KB.

What do I need?

A browser from 2023 or later: Chrome 103, Safari 16.4, Firefox 113, or newer.

report a post

If a link shows something that shouldn’t be up, send it to report@watchmetype.com. We can stop a reported post from playing on this site.
