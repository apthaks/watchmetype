about watchmetype

let them watch your words arrive.

how it works

Write up to 500 characters, then share a link. Anyone who opens it can watch your post being typed—every pause, typo and edit.

Pasting is off. Deleted words stay in the replay.

a witness, not a proof

A replay doesn’t prove who wrote the words, whether they’re original, or whether AI was used. Recordings can be constructed.

privacy

The recording lives in the link. Your draft is saved in this browser.

The recording is stored after the #, which your browser doesn’t send to our server. There are no accounts, no post database and no analytics.

Recent shared posts are also saved in this browser. Anyone with the full link, including an app you share it through, can access the recording.

editing

Open your own link in the same browser and choose “continue editing”. Sharing again creates a new link. The old link stays the same, and shared links can’t be recalled.

report a post

Send the full link to report@watchmetype.com. We can block playback on this site, but can’t erase copies of the recording.
