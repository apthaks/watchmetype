watchmetype

about

Write a post of up to 500 characters and tap share. You get your text plus a link. Anyone who opens the link watches the post being typed, at the pace you typed it: every pause, typo and fix.

what gets recorded

Each time the text changes, the page notes what was added or removed, where, and how long it had been since the last change. That is all. Pauses longer than three seconds are kept as a single long pause, so a reader sees “paused 3 min” instead of waiting three minutes.

Pasting and dropping text are switched off, and so is autocorrect replacing a word for you. If your keyboard inserts a whole word at once, by swipe or by suggestion, the replay shows the whole word arriving at once, because that is what happened.

witnessed, not proven

A replay shows that the text was typed, one edit after another. It doesn’t show who typed it, and it can’t rule out someone retyping words they got elsewhere. Read it as a witness, not as proof.

where your post lives

In the link, and nowhere else. The whole recording is packed into the part of the address after the #, which browsers never send to a server. There are no accounts, no database and no analytics.

That also means a link can’t be edited or taken back once you’ve sent it, and if an app cuts a long link short, the replay won’t open.

editing your own post

Your draft and the posts you’ve shared are remembered in this browser only. Open one of your own links here and you’ll see “continue editing”: new edits are added to the end of the recording, and sharing gives you a new link. The old link keeps showing the old version. On another browser or device you’re a reader like anyone else.

reporting a post

If a link shows something that shouldn’t be up, send the link to report@watchmetype.com. We can stop a reported post from playing on this site.

what you need

A browser from 2023 or later: Chrome 103, Safari 16.4, Firefox 113, or newer.
